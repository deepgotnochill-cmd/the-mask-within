## The Mask Within 1.1 · Windows

- The hunter investigates sounds and searches your last known position. Break sight around a corner, then press **H** beside either bedroom bed to hide.
- Running carries farther than walking. Crouching is quiet, and a heartbeat signals a chase.
- The stair crank saves progress at each third of a turn. A meter shows when you can let go safely. Nearby prompts guide the key and attic-door interactions.
- Title, pause, controls, retry and escape screens now work with mouse or keyboard. Pausing freezes the game and its audio.

Extract the entire Windows ZIP, then run **The Mask Within.exe** from inside its folder.
